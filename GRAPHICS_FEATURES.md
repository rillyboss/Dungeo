# 🎨 Cool Graphics Features Added to the RPG

## ASCII Art Graphics Throughout the Game

### 🎭 Character Class Art
When you create a character, you'll see unique ASCII art for each class:
- **⚔️ Warrior**: Armored figure with sword and shield
- **🔮 Mage**: Mystical figure with stars and magic
- **🗡️ Rogue**: Stealthy figure with dagger

### ⚔️ Combat Graphics
- **Battle Start**: Epic "BATTLE BEGINS!" banner when combat starts
- **Enemy Appearances**: Different ASCII art based on enemy type:
  - Dragons/Whelps 🐉
  - Skeletons 💀
  - Goblins/Orcs 👹
  - Generic monsters 👾

### 💥 Combat Effects
- **Critical Hits**: Explosive banner when you land a critical hit
- **Ability Animations**: Unique visuals for different ability types:
  - 🔥 Fire abilities (Fireball, Battle Rage)
  - ❄️ Ice abilities (Ice Lance)
  - 💚💀 Shadow/Poison abilities (Shadow Step, Poison Blade)
  - 🛡️ Defensive abilities (Shield Wall)
  - ⚡ Generic abilities (Power Strike, etc.)

### 🏆 Victory & Defeat
- **Victory Screen**: Large "VICTORY" banner with trophy ASCII art
- **Defeat Screen**: "DEFEATED" banner with skull art
- **Level Up**: Spectacular "LEVEL UP!" banner with stars and sparkles

### 💰 Rewards
- **Gold Rewards**: Animated gold coin display
- **Loot Drops**: Treasure chest with sparkles animation

### 🏪 Locations
- **Shop**: Detailed merchant shop building art
- **Rest Area**: Peaceful scene with sleeping character, clouds, stars, and "ZZZ"

## Visual Enhancements

All graphics are:
- ✅ Color-coded for easy reading
- ✅ Animated with timing delays for dramatic effect
- ✅ Contextual (change based on abilities, enemies, outcomes)
- ✅ Non-intrusive (enhance without cluttering gameplay)

## How to Experience

Simply play through the game and you'll see these graphics appear:
1. Create a character → See class art
2. Enter combat → Battle start banner + enemy art
3. Use abilities → Ability-specific animations
4. Land crits → Critical hit banner
5. Win/lose → Victory/defeat screens
6. Visit shop → Shop building art
7. Rest → Peaceful rest scene

The graphics make the console RPG feel much more alive and engaging!
